ULTIMATE FOOTBALL 3D

Welcome to your 3D football game.

Landscape mode recommended. Internet is required to load the 3D library.

Step 2: Run the game 1. Save the file as "index.html". 2. Open its preview. 3. Make sure your phone has an internet connection so Three.js can load. 4. Turn your phone sideways. 5. Select your team and formation. 6. Press START MATCH. Controls - Joystick: move your footballer. - PASS: pass or kick the ball. - SHOOT: shoot towards goal. - RUN: sprint. - SWITCH: control the player nearest the ball. - PAUSE: pause the match. Important: This is a playable 3D foundation, not yet a finished Football League 2026-level game. The next major improvement should be proper animated 3D footballers, followed by more intelligent AI and goalkeeper saves.
